Our story

Built on trust, one honest sale at a time

Sakatan Business Ventures started as a small stand in Computer Village, Lagos State, with one goal: sell people phones and laptops they don't have to worry about the day after they pay.

Years later, we're still on the same street — now trusted by returning customers, referrals and even other vendors who send their own customers our way when they need something specific.

We deal mainly in iPhones and Samsung devices, alongside laptops and the accessories that keep them running — chargers, earbuds, screen guards, power banks and more.
